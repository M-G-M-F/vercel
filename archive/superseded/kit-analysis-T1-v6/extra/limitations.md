T1 limitations

Recorded oxygen is not a limiting oxygen index. No material hazard ranking or causal comparison. Sample labels may be reused. PSI-26 oxygen unit conversion is inferred; airflow is not standardised across investigations. Date corrections marked provisional require original records; no claim of scientific verification. Raw videos were not analysed.

V6: contextual material labels are hypotheses; numeric airflow sequences are display entries, not timing or extinction data. CO negatives and censoring are not exact concentrations; oxygen screening sensitivity is not a physical threshold. RAG retrieval fixtures have not been evaluated against a deployed retriever.
